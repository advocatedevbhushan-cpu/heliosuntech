Laboratory Rigor

Four Pillars of Quality Verification

Continuous testing protocols ensure high mechanical and optical uniformity across every dispatched pallet.

TEST 01

MFI Calibration

Melt Flow Index testing verifies melt viscosity under ASTM D1238 standards, matching injection moulding cycle times.

TEST 02

Density & Moisture

Moisture levels calibrated below 0.20% with precision moisture analyzer and gradient density immersion testing.

TEST 03

Surface Energy

Corona treatment verification via dyne testing pens ensuring optimal adhesion for coating and lamination applications.

TEST 04

Archive Retention

180-day physical lot sample archiving for continuous customer traceability and comparative analytical verification.
