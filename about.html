Corporate Background & Mission

Built Around the Future of Sustainable Materials.

Heliosuntech Plastics Private Limited is establishing an integrated industrial circular platform — recovering valuable hydrocarbons from plastic waste streams, manufacturing calibrated granules, and producing precision industrial coating films.

Core Operating Model

An Integrated Three-Tier Approach.

We bridge the gap between post-industrial waste recovery and high-precision downstream polymer manufacturing.

PILLAR 01

RECLAIM

Plastic waste streams are systematically collected, decontaminated, and optically segregated by polymer classification, preserving the core hydrocarbon value of industrial plastics.

PILLAR 02

REPROCESS

Recovered polymers are converted into consistent, usable plastic granules through twin-screw melt-filtration, vacuum degassing, and calibrated strand/underwater pelletization.

PILLAR 03

RECREATE

Refined materials move directly into downstream industrial applications, including high-precision plastic coating film and engineered plastic components.

Plant & Operations

Manufacturing Platform With Purpose.

Located at Ecotech-III, Greater Noida, our manufacturing ecosystem is engineered for polymer thermal stability, process repeatability, and minimal material variance.

01

Material Recovery

Direct intake, shredding, and friction washing of post-industrial polymer waste streams, preparing high-purity flakes for thermal conversion.

02

Thermal Processing

Twin-screw extrusion with progressive temperature barrel zones and dual vacuum venting to purge volatile moisture and entrapped gases.

03

Granulation & Pelletizing

Die-face underwater and strand pelletizing systems that produce uniform polymer granules engineered for downstream injection and blow-moulding.

04

Film Extrusion

Advanced blown and cast chill-roll extrusion producing precision plastic coating films with calibrated gauge control and surface energy treatment.

05

Quality Assurance

Batch-level melt-flow indexing, density monitoring, and surface tension verification ensuring strict adherence to industrial processing standards.

06

Logistics & Packaging

Moisture-barrier multi-wall bagging, palletized stretch-wrapping, and bulk jumbo storage tailored for seamless logistics to industrial partners.

Circularity Model

Circularity Built Into Every Process.

Sustainability at Heliosuntech is not a marketing narrative — it is an engineering discipline. We reintroduce discarded polymers directly into industrial utility cycles.

The Closed-Loop Philosophy

Plastic waste is an unharvested resource. Through rigorous decontamination, molecular homogenization, and precision film extrusion, we ensure polymers circulate repeatedly before reaching thermal recovery.
