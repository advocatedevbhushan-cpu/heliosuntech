Process Architecture

The Six-Stage Transformation Pipeline.

Rigorous industrial stages turning raw plastic scrap into certified high-grade output.

01

Inbound Scrap Audit

Polymer identification, sorting, and contamination inspection.

02

Shred & Wash

Heavy granulation followed by multi-cycle friction hot washing.

03

Flake Sorting

High-speed optical classification ensuring polymer purity.

04

Compounding

Degasification, melt-filtration, and twin-screw pelletizing.

05

Film Extrusion

Blown & cast multi-layer precision film manufacturing.

06

QA & Dispatch

ASTM-certified batch testing and traceable logistics dispatch.
